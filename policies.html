Our policies

Delivery, returns, privacy and terms — written in plain words, not legal language. Last updated: 2 November 2026.

Student project notice. This website is a coursework project for BUC3106 at Makerere University Business School. It collects no customer information, takes no payments and sends no messages. These policies describe how the business would operate if it traded online.

1. Delivery

  • Order cut-off: orders placed before 4:00 pm are delivered the same day. Orders after 4:00 pm are delivered the next morning.
  • Zones and fees: Zone A (Kitintale, Ntinda) UGX 2,000 · Zone B (Namuwongo, Nakawa) UGX 3,000 · Zone C (Kampala CBD) UGX 5,000 · Zone D (Bukoto, Kisaasi, Kololo) UGX 6,000.
  • Free delivery on orders above UGX 60,000 in Kitintale and Ntinda.
  • Business standing orders (cafés, schools, offices) are delivered before 8:00 am on the agreed mornings.
  • Delays: heavy rain, traffic or a power cut can delay us. If we expect a delay of more than 30 minutes, we message you before your delivery time.
  • Nobody home: the rider waits 10 minutes and calls you. If there is no answer, we return the next slot and charge the delivery fee once more only if the second trip is a different zone.
  • Check at the door: please check quantity and condition before the rider leaves. Damaged or wrong items reported within 2 hours are replaced free.

2. Returns and refunds

  • Wrong, damaged or spoiled item → tell us within 2 hours with a photo. We remake it or refund it in full.
  • We cannot deliver → full refund, including the delivery fee, within 3 working days.
  • Custom cake cancelled 48 hours or more ahead → full refund. Less than 48 hours → 50% refund, because ingredients were already bought.
  • Refunds go back through the same method you paid with — never to a different number or account.
  • Because of hygiene, bread, cakes and pastries cannot be returned once delivered and accepted, unless the item is wrong, damaged or not as described.

3. Privacy notice

This notice follows Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. It explains in plain words what we do with your information.

What we collect

Your name, your phone number, your delivery area or address, what you ordered, and — only if you pay — the transaction ID or payment reference needed to confirm the payment. This website itself collects nothing: there is no sign-up form, no login, no cookie and no database behind these pages.

Why we collect it

  • To prepare and deliver your order correctly
  • To confirm that a payment reached our account
  • To answer questions and handle refunds or complaints
  • To keep financial records for the time required by Ugandan tax law

Who can see it

Only the two or three Sweet Moments staff who take orders and deliver them. We do not sell, rent or share your number with advertisers. We disclose information only where the law requires it, for example a court order or a request from a tax authority.

How long we keep it

WhatsApp order messages: 24 months. Financial and payment records: 5 years, as required for tax records. After that we delete them.

Your rights

  • Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything wrong
  • Ask us to delete what we hold, and we delete it unless the law says we must keep it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time by messaging us

To use any of these rights, write to sweetbakeries@gmail.com or message +256 750 568 522. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Office set up under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

4. Terms of service (short version)

  • Prices: the price shown on the Products page is the price you pay. Delivery is added as listed. Prices are in Uganda shillings and may change after a supplier price change; the website is updated the same week.
  • Orders: an order is confirmed only when you receive a written confirmation on WhatsApp or by email. A quote on its own is not a confirmation.
  • Availability: everything is baked to order. If we sell out, we tell you before you pay and offer a swap or a full refund.
  • Custom cakes: need 48 hours' notice. What we write in the WhatsApp confirmation (flavour, size, message on the cake) is what we bake.
  • Allergens: our kitchen handles wheat, eggs, milk and nuts. Tell us about an allergy before ordering; we cannot promise a nut-free kitchen.
  • Liability: if something goes wrong, our responsibility is limited to the value of that order.
  • Complaints: message the number on this site or email us. We reply within one working day. If we cannot agree, the laws of Uganda apply, and disputes are handled in the courts of Kampala.
  • Our content: the design, text and logo of this site belong to the student group. Photographs are used under free licences — sources are listed in our Rules Sheet.
