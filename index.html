Our story

It started with one oven in Kitintale

Sweet Moments Bakery began when a family in Kitintale started baking extra loaves for the neighbours. The loaves finished faster than they could bake them.

Today we bake bread, cookies, cupcakes, birthday cakes, pastries and muffins for households, and we supply trays of bread and cupcakes to cafés, schools and offices around Nakawa division. Everything is still mixed by hand in the same kitchen.

  • Real kitchen, real bakers, real ingredients you can pronounce
  • Wholesale prices for cafés, schools, offices and event planners
