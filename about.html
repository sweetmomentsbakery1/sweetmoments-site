From one family oven to the whole of Kitintale

We are a small neighbourhood bakery that believes fresh food should not need a middleman — you order, we bake, we deliver.

How it started

An extra tray of loaves for the neighbours

Sweet Moments Bakery started in a home kitchen in Kitintale, where the family baked more bread than it could eat and gave the rest to neighbours.

The neighbours stopped saying "thank you" and started saying "can I buy two more?". That was the moment the kitchen became a business. The first product was bread. Cookies and mandazi followed, then cupcakes for the children on the lane, then birthday cakes for the whole estate.

Today the bakery serves three kinds of customers: households who order on WhatsApp, businesses — cafés, schools and offices that take a standing delivery before 8:00 am, and event planners who order cupcakes and cakes in bulk.

The journey

Four steps to today

Step 1 · The home oven

Weekend baking for family and immediate neighbours in Kitintale.

Step 2 · The first orders

Neighbours begin paying for bread and mandazi. The bakery opens at 6:00 am so bread is ready before school and work.

Step 3 · Wholesale customers

Two cafés and one school in Nakawa start taking standing morning deliveries of bread and cupcakes.

Step 4 · Going online

Orders now arrive through WhatsApp and this website, so customers can see the full price list before they buy.

Our values

What we will not compromise on

01

Fresh, never frozen

Everything is baked the morning it is sold. If a batch does not sell, we do not sell it the next day.

02

Published prices

The price you read here is the price you pay. Delivery fees are listed openly on the Policies page.

03

Local first

We buy flour, eggs and margarine from suppliers in Kampala and we hire from Kitintale.
