How to pay & order

Five ways to pay, three steps each. Read the box marked SAMPLE — this is a student project, so no money can move through this page.

This is a student project for BUC3106 at MUBS. Payments are not active. The bank account numbers, till numbers and card links below are obvious sample numbers marked SAMPLE. No real money is collected on this website and no customer details are stored on this website. When a real shop opens, it replaces these numbers with the shop's real, verified details.

Before you pay

Ordering takes three steps

Nothing is paid on the website. You order, we confirm, then you pay using one of the methods below.

01

Send your order

Open WhatsApp and send the item codes, quantities and your delivery area. We reply with the total, including delivery.

02

We confirm the total

You get a written confirmation: products, quantity, delivery fee, total and the expected delivery time.

03

Pay, then we bake

Pay with one of the five methods below and send the transaction ID. We confirm it from our own records and start baking.

Payment confirmation

How we confirm that you paid

  • We check our own records, not your screenshot. A screenshot on its own is never accepted as proof.
  • For mobile money, we open our own MoMo or Airtel menu and read the money in our transaction history using the transaction ID you sent.
  • For bank transfers, we match the amount, date and reference against our own bank statement.
  • For card payments, we wait for the payment provider's own confirmation email — not a picture of one.
  • If the money is not in our records within 30 minutes, we pause the order and ask you to check with your network.
Refunds

If something goes wrong

Our refund rule

  • Wrong or damaged item: tell us within 2 hours of delivery with a photo — we remake or refund in full.
  • We cannot deliver: a full refund of everything paid, including the delivery fee, within 3 working days.
  • Custom cake cancelled 48+ hours ahead: full refund. Less than 48 hours: 50% refund, because ingredients were already bought.
  • Cash on delivery returned at the door: pay nothing; the delivery fee for that trip is not charged.
  • Refunds are sent back through the same method you paid with — never to a different number or account.

Refunds are how this project says it would work. No money can move while this is a student project.

Our reasoning

Why these methods — and why no cryptocurrency

The half-page explanation that goes with this page, in two cards.

01

Chosen for the customers we actually have

Most of our orders are small amounts paid from a phone, so MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money come first: they work on any handset, need no bank account and settle into our account in seconds, so baking can start immediately. Cash on delivery protects the first-time buyer who has never met us — you check the items at your gate before money changes hands. Bank transfer exists for cafés, schools and offices, who need an invoice they can file for their accounts. Card payment is kept for large event orders through a licensed provider, and the customer types the card number on the provider's own page — never on ours.

02

Why we do not accept cryptocurrency

The Bank of Uganda has stated that cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin are not legal tender in Uganda, that it does not issue or regulate them, and it has warned the public that anyone who buys or sells them does so at their own risk. Payment providers that move money in Uganda must be licensed by the Bank of Uganda. Because we could not honour the refund promise on our Policies page in an unregulated token — nor show URA where the income came from — Sweet Moments Bakery accepts mobile money, cash on delivery, bank transfer and cards through a licensed provider only.
